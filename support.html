Support

Questions or a problem? Email toolsfolioapp@gmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two working days.

How do I restore Toolsfolio Pro on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Toolsfolio, go to Settings, tap Unlock Every Tool, then Restore Purchases. You'll need an internet connection for this step.

Does Pro work without internet?

Yes. Your purchase is checked on your iPhone, so every Pro tool keeps working offline. You only need a connection to buy, restore or renew.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Toolsfolio. Cancel at least a day before it renews. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

Why don't I see the Smart tools?

Smart tools use Apple Intelligence, which needs iOS 26 and an iPhone 15 Pro or newer. On other iPhones they're hidden. If your iPhone supports it, turn on Apple Intelligence in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri.

What happens to my Private Vault if I delete the app?

Deleting Toolsfolio deletes the Vault and everything in it. Its files are encrypted on your iPhone only, with no copy anywhere else, so export anything you want to keep first.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
